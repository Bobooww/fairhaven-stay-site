The Chalet

Catskills Luxury Chalet

Pine on every surface, and one whole wall given to the trees.

The main room is a study in pine — walls, ceiling and the steep raked soffit above the sofa, all boarded in the same honey-coloured timber, with a soft olive green showing where the boarding stops.

Light arrives from one side only: a stack of windows running floor to eaves, straight into the trees. An open stair with a steel cable balustrade climbs past the seating to a railed loft.
